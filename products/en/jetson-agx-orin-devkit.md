# Jetson AGX Orin Developer Kit (64GB)

> **[Buy in Store](https://www.juxitech.com)**

## Overview

The NVIDIA® Jetson AGX Orin™ Developer Kit is the flagship developer kit of
the Jetson Orin family — a compact AI computer for developing robotics,
computer-vision, and generative-AI applications at the edge. **Juxi Technology
ships the official NVIDIA kit** with complete, verified documentation.

Highlights:

- **Up to 275 TOPS** of AI performance with power configurable between 15W and 60W *(NVIDIA)*
- **2048-core NVIDIA Ampere architecture GPU with 64 Tensor Cores** *(NVIDIA)*
- **Ships pre-flashed** — eMMC comes with an L4T BSP image; boot straight into Ubuntu out of the box
- **Emulates other Jetson Orin modules** — re-flash to emulate AGX Orin, Orin NX, or Orin Nano performance/power characteristics *(NVIDIA User Guide)*
- **Current software: JetPack 7.2.1** (Jetson Linux 39.2.1, Ubuntu 24.04, CUDA 13.2.2) — update path is the Jetson ISO, no host PC required

**Use cases**: on-device LLM/agentic AI, multi-stream video analytics, robotics
perception, edge generative AI.

## Getting Started

1. Connect a DisplayPort monitor, USB keyboard and mouse
2. Connect the included USB-C power supply (port above the DC jack) — the kit powers on automatically
3. Boot into Ubuntu and complete the first-time setup
4. Update to JetPack 7.2.1 with the Jetson ISO (USB stick, no host PC needed)
